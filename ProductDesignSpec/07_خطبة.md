# KOSHA Product Specification: 07 - خطبة (Khitbah) Matchmaking Ecosystem

## Vision
خطبة (Khitbah) is KOSHA's anonymized, privacy-first matchmaking ecosystem. It fundamentally rejects shallow dating app tropes (swiping left/right, superficial photo cards, public directory browsing). Instead, Khitbah offers a dignified, family-respecting compatibility matrix where anonymized candidate profiles are presented through editorial aesthetic framing, multi-dimensional cultural values, and moderated connection requests.

## User Goals
- Discover deeply compatible life partners based on shared family values, religious harmony, career aspirations, and lifestyle expectations.
- Maintain absolute privacy and anonymity until mutual consent and family involvement are established.
- Send and receive connection requests in a secure, respectful environment free of harassment.

## UX Problems
- Typical dating apps gamify human connection with addictive swipe gestures and superficial image rating.
- Public candidate lists compromise user privacy and social reputation within tight-knit communities.
- Lack of structural family-involved communication mechanics.

## Proposed Solution
- **Anonymized Identity Cards**: Profiles are identified by unique aesthetic IDs (e.g. *Soul ID: K-842*) featuring abstract portrait frames with soft artistic depth filters.
- **The Compatibility Matrix Wheel**: An interactive multi-axis visual wheel displaying percentage alignment across 5 core pillars: *Family Values, Faith & Life Path, Career & Ambition, Financial Philosophy, Lifestyle & Hobbies*.
- **Private Connection Protocol**: Communication cannot be initiated via direct message; users send formal "Khitbah Connection Requests" that require mutual approval and guardian (Wali) notification options.
- **Section Accent Identity**: Soft Lavender (`#9F86C0`) highlight accents anchored on Warm Cream (`#FAF7F2`) background.

## Design Concepts
- **Soul Alignment Chart**: A custom SVG radar web chart with soft lavender fill gradient, rendering compatibility score (`94% Match`).
- **Arch-Contoured Aesthetic Framing**: Candidate photos displayed with soft artistic diffusion within cathedral arch shapes (`120px` top radius).

## Component Behaviour
- **Matrix Wheel Tap**: Tapping any dimension on the compatibility wheel expands detailed Q&A responses for that pillar.
- **Send Khitbah Request**: Button press morphs into a confirmation modal with optional Guardian (Wali) email notification input field.

## Animation Behaviour
- **Radar Path Interpolation**: When switching candidate profiles, the radar mesh smoothly animates its vertex positions over `350ms`.
- **Card Flip Transition**: Tapping candidate portrait gently flips card to expose verified education and background credentials.

## Accessibility
- **Radar Data Text Equivalence**: Screen readers read out clear verbal summaries: "Candidate K-842: Overall compatibility 94 percent. High alignment in Faith and Values."
- **Privacy Mode Focus**: Audio cues confirm when screen is operating under strict anonymized privacy mode.

## Edge Cases
- **Subscription Expiry**: Expired paywall gracefully overlays profile card with a soft blurred glass pane prompting subscription renewal without exposing candidate private data.

## AI Suggestions
- **AI Compatibility Matchmaker**: Natural language processing analyzes personal bio text and lifestyle answers to calculate deep cultural compatibility beyond simple numeric tags.

## Developer Notes
- Candidate images fetched as encrypted token URLs `GET /api/khitbah/candidates/{id}/avatar?token=...`.
- Candidate IDs obfuscated on client; real user names never exposed in client bundle.

## Fable Implementation Notes
- Use `<KhitbahProfileContainer candidateId={id} accent="#9F86C0" />`.
