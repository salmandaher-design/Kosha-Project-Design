# KOSHA Product Specification: 09 - زفة (Zaffa) Interactive Dream Roadmap

## Vision
زفة (Zaffa) is the crown jewel and signature feature of KOSHA. It completely replaces traditional wedding planning checklists, spreadsheets, and project management apps. Instead of treating wedding planning as a stressful logistical task, Zaffa transforms it into **The Interactive Dream Roadmap**—an award-worthy, visual, luxury storyline where the couple watches their dream wedding literally come alive step-by-step.

Every reservation, contract sign-off, guest confirmation, and decor choice updates the visual roadmap arc. Completing a milestone unlocks the next chapter of the journey with celebratory gold particle effects and emotional narrative copy.

## User Goals
- Experience wedding planning as an interactive, joyous story arc rather than an overwhelming list of tasks.
- Track 15 interconnected wedding pillars: *Venue, Photography, Decoration, Flowers, Music & Zaffa Troupe, Transportation, Guest Management, Budget, Timeline, Invitations, Tasks, Contracts, Payments, Checklists, Vendor Coordination*.
- Receive real-time AI risk alerts (e.g. weather forecast warnings for outdoor venues, missing contract dependencies, overdue payments).

## UX Problems
- Traditional event planning tools look like Trello or Excel—cold, overwhelming, and uninspiring.
- Users miss critical vendor dependency deadlines (e.g., booking flowers before locking venue stage dimensions).
- Lack of holistic connection between budget, vendor contracts, guest lists, and weather conditions.

## Proposed Solution
- **The Interactive Dream Roadmap Arc**: A dynamic, winding continuous ribbon path spanning across the screen. Milestone nodes (Gold Rings) sit along the path representing key pillars.
- **Node State Progression**:
  1. *Locked Node*: Translucent grey ring; unlocks when prerequisite dependencies are met.
  2. *Active Node*: Pulsing Deep Burgundy ring with gold halo; currently in progress.
  3. *Completed Milestone Node*: Solid gold ring (`#D4AF37`) with glowing checkmark; unveils milestone memory card.
- **Integrated Zaffa Modules**:
  - **Venue & Stage Studio**: 3D spatial stage layouts, seating capacity maps, lighting presets.
  - **Photography & Cinema Storyboard**: Visual mood boards linked to photographer shot-lists.
  - **Floral & Decor Atelier**: Color theme synchronizer with real-time supplier quotes.
  - **Traditional Zaffa Music Troupe Selector**: Audio snippet player for traditional Emirati folklore bands.
  - **Luxury Transport Fleet**: Royal car booking and arrival timeline coordination.
  - **Guest Experience Matrix**: Interactive seating arrangement tables, RSVPs, dietary needs, digital invitations.
  - **Real-Time Budget & Risk Engine**: Visual budget wheel tracking committed vs spent funds, auto-flagging weather risks and vendor overlaps.

## Design Concepts
- **Continuous Ribbon Roadmap Arc**: An SVG bezier stroke that loops gracefully down the screen, responding to vertical touch drags.
- **Section Accent Identity**: Deep Velvet Burgundy (`#4A0D15`) foundation with shimmering Gold (`#D4AF37`) milestone nodes.

## Component Behaviour
- **Node Tap**: Tapping any roadmap node smoothly expands its detail workspace inline while centering the node in the viewport.
- **Complete Step Button**: Pressing "Complete Step" triggers celebratory gold foil particle confetti, plays soft success chime, and draws ribbon path connecting to the next node.

## Animation Behaviour
- **Ribbon Draw Physics**: Completing a step animates `stroke-dashoffset` over `1200ms` with spring settling physics.
- **Node Pulse Effect**: Active node glows with a continuous breathing aura (`scale: 1.0` to `1.08` over `2000ms`).

## Accessibility
- **Node Voiceover Readout**: Screen reader speaks: "Roadmap Node 3 of 15: Floral and Decor Atelier. Status: Active. Budget: 45,000 dirhams spent out of 60,000."
- **Alternative Linear View**: Provides toggle to switch from curved roadmap arc to accessible structured list view.

## Edge Cases
- **Weather / Vendor Risk Detected**: Risk radar flashes soft warning badge on top right; tapping node unveils mitigation suggestions (e.g. "Rain probability 40% on Nov 14: Tap to view covered marquee options").

## AI Suggestions
- **AI Wedding Concierge (Karakoaz)**: Continuously evaluates contract terms, vendor timelines, and budget balances, automatically suggesting optimization actions to prevent cost overruns.

## Developer Notes
- Roadmap graph topology stored in JSON tree structure with node dependencies `dependsOn: ["node_1", "node_2"]`.
- Confetti particles powered by high-performance GPU canvas canvas renderer.

## Fable Implementation Notes
- Use `<ZaffaDreamRoadmap graphData={tree} activeNodeId={id} accent="#4A0D15" />`.
