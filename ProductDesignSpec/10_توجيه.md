# KOSHA Product Specification: 10 - توجيه (Tawjeeh) Knowledge & Consultation Sanctuary

## Vision
توجيه (Tawjeeh) transforms counseling and educational guidance into a high-luxury knowledge platform. It moves beyond standard appointment booking into an inspiring sanctuary featuring live webinars, online workshops, physical seminars, closed members-only events, expert-authored editorial publications, and private 1-on-1 consultations with verified marital, legal, and psychological counselors.

## User Goals
- Access verified expert knowledge on pre-marital preparation, legal marriage contracts, financial management, and family harmony.
- Register for live interactive webinars, local seminars, and private expert Q&A sessions with countdown reminders.
- Book confidential private consultations via high-definition video rooms or scheduled physical appointments.

## UX Problems
- Consultation apps look like sterile medical booking portals.
- Educational content is buried in unstructured blog lists.
- Users feel intimidated when seeking marital advice or psychological counseling.

## Proposed Solution
- **The Knowledge Sanctuary Hub**: Editorial magazine layout split into three fluid pillars: *Live Knowledge Events, Expert Library, Private Consultations*.
- **Live Event Cards**: High-fashion banners featuring countdown timers, speaker bio cards, interactive registration buttons, and map pin integration for physical seminars.
- **The Knowledge Center**: Curated articles, audio podcasts, and video masterclasses categorized by life stage (*Pre-Marriage Harmony, Legal Rights & Contracts, Financial Synergy, Emotional Connection*).
- **Private Consultation Studio**: Seamless 1-on-1 scheduling with interactive time-slot grids, attachment upload, and private status tracking.
- **Section Accent Identity**: Royal Blue (`#1A365D`) highlight accents on Warm Cream (`#FAF7F2`) background.

## Design Concepts
- **Countdown Timer Badges**: Live events display animated flip-digit countdown timers (`02d : 14h : 35m`) in Cormorant Garamond.
- **Speaker Spotlight Spheres**: Circular avatars framed with royal blue hairline rings and verified expert credentials.

## Component Behaviour
- **Register for Webinar**: Tap updates button state to "Registered" with tick checkmark and automatically prompts add-to-calendar dialog.
- **Time Slot Selection**: Tapping an available hour chip highlights chip in solid Royal Blue (`#1A365D`) and unveils private consultation form.

## Animation Behaviour
- **Countdown Digit Flip**: Timer numbers transition down with a soft vertical flip spring animation every minute.
- **Masterclass Video Expand**: Tapping a masterclass card expands video player inline using shared element transition.

## Accessibility
- **Audio Captions & Transcripts**: All video masterclasses include synchronized closed captions and downloadable text transcripts.
- **Accessible Time Slots**: Slot selection readable via VoiceOver ("Available slot: 4:00 PM to 5:00 PM, Thursday").

## Edge Cases
- **Urgent Consultation Request**: Urgent mode shifts priority to top, displaying "Immediate Specialist Available" queue for high-priority guidance.

## AI Suggestions
- **Personalized Knowledge Curator**: AI analyzes user's active stage in Khitbah/Zaffa and recommends relevant Tawjeeh articles (e.g. suggesting marriage contract legal tips when user reaches Zaffa contract milestone).

## Developer Notes
- Video streams powered by HLS / WebRTC signaling socket `ws://signaling/live`.
- Webinar registrations synchronized with user calendar via iCal / Google Calendar APIs.

## Fable Implementation Notes
- Use `<TawjeehSanctuaryHub events={liveEvents} articles={knowledgeBase} accent="#1A365D" />`.
