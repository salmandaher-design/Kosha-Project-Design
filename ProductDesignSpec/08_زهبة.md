# KOSHA Product Specification: 08 - زهبة (Zahba) Bridal Trousseau Studio

## Vision
زهبة (Zahba) is the luxury bridal trousseau and prep studio in KOSHA. Zahba honors the sacred Emirati tradition of preparing the bride’s wedding trousseau—attire, fine jewelry, haute couture gowns, incense (Bakhour), perfumes, and custom invitation suites. It converts generic e-commerce catalog shopping into an editorial luxury atelier.

## User Goals
- Curate, customize, and purchase bespoke bridal items from top Emirati and international luxury designers.
- Organize items into personalized "Zahba Chest Collections" shared with family members.
- Request custom tailoring appointments and private atelier viewings.

## UX Problems
- E-commerce apps look like commodity discount stores with dense product grids and red sale badges.
- Lack of emotional celebratory atmosphere during high-value luxury purchases.
- Fragmented order tracking across multiple high-end boutique suppliers.

## Proposed Solution
- **The Editorial Atelier Gallery**: Products are displayed inside Arch-Contoured Editorial Frames with high-resolution photography, gold-foil price badges in AED, and artisan story snippets.
- **The Digital Zahba Chest**: A visual digital chest container where saved and purchased items assemble like a physical luxury dowry trunk.
- **Bespoke Atelier Booking**: Integrated intake form allowing brides to request private tailor fittings, fabric samples, and custom embroidery consultations.
- **Section Accent Identity**: Warm Rose Gold (`#D4AF37`) highlight accents on Warm Cream (`#FAF7F2`) background.

## Design Concepts
- **Gold Foil Sheen Badges**: Price tags rendered with metallic gold gradient shimmer borders (`#D4AF37` to `#F3E5AB`).
- **Interactive Trousseau Progress Bar**: Displays completion percentage across 6 traditional Zahba categories (Jewelry, Gowns, Perfumes, Fabrics, Footwear, Abayas).

## Component Behaviour
- **Add to Zahba Chest**: Button tap triggers dynamic particle sparkle animation (`✦`) flowing from product card directly into top chest icon counter.
- **Category Switcher**: Sliding between categories smoothly filters product layout without full page refresh.

## Animation Behaviour
- **Gold Sheen Shimmer**: Price badges feature a continuous diagonal shimmer light sweep every `4000ms`.
- **Chest Open Expansion**: Opening "My Zahba Chest" displays a 3D isometric chest lid opening animation unveiling collected bridal assets.

## Accessibility
- **High-Resolution Zoom Contrast**: Product gallery images support pinch-to-zoom with screen reader accessibility descriptions for tactile fabric textures.
- **Currency Readability**: Screen readers speak prices clearly: "18,500 United Arab Emirates Dirhams."

## Edge Cases
- **Bespoke Custom Made Items**: For items with no standard size, "Add to Cart" button automatically morphs to "Schedule Measurement Consultation".

## AI Suggestions
- **Color Palette Harmonizer**: AI inspects saved gowns and suggests matching jewelry sets, footwear, and bridal scents that complement the bride's chosen color palette.

## Developer Notes
- Product catalog synchronized with inventory API `GET /api/zahba/products`.
- High-res images pre-cached using progressive WebP / AVIF formats.

## Fable Implementation Notes
- Use `<ZahbaAtelierGallery products={data} accent="#D4AF37" />`.
